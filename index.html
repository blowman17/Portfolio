About Me

Who is Philip Asante?

I'm a B.Sc Information Technology student at the University of Cape Coast, Ghana, chasing First Class honors while building production software on the side. Combining solid full-stack engineering principles with modern AI-augmented workflows, I turn complex ideas into actual systems built for real businesses.

My work spans the full stack: Java and Spring Boot on the backend, Node.js and Express when I need to move fast, React on the front end, and PostgreSQL, MySQL, or SQLite depending on what the job calls for. Outside the IDE, I run Purple Juice, a clothing brand, and I'm active in ministry and administrative work with my church's Young People's Guild.
